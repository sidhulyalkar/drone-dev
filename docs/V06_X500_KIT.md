# AeroForge v0.6 | Holybro X500 V2 reference hardware

This implementation creates a source-backed **reference kit**, not a bill of materials authorized for procurement or a physical aircraft release. It does not alter the separate Source Two FPV reference.

## Evidence-backed manufacturer facts

- X500 V2 frame: 500 mm class, main carbon plates 144 x 144 mm, 16 x 16 mm motor mount pattern; Holybro publishes original CAD separately.
- PX4 development kit options: Pixhawk 6C with analog PM02 V3, or Pixhawk 6X with digital PM02D (check exact ordered package).
- ARF kit: 2216 KV920 motors (4), BLHeli S 20A ESCs (4, described as compatible with 4S), 1045 props, battery XT60 and ESC XT30 PDB.
- 4S LiPo battery is separately sourced. Charging equipment and ground RC may also be separate.
- Store and older guide vary on GNSS M10 versus M8N, and connector terminology (MR30/XT30). These are unresolved revision-specific facts, not automatic compatibility passes.

The manufacturer's digital PM02D product page explicitly excludes Pixhawk 6C, which expects the analog power module. The engine fails this pairing as well as a non-4S battery selection against the stated kit ESC compatibility. Even a matched kit variant remains unqualified for operation.

## Simulation scope

In a properly installed separate PX4-Autopilot checkout on a supported system, PX4 documents the baseline command:

    make px4_sitl gz_x500

The upstream models also include depth camera and visual odometry variants. This is a **generic upstream PX4 model**, not an executable custom calibrated model of the Holybro X500 V2. AeroForge exports a blocked custom simulation contract with unknown mass/inertia/rotor coefficients as null. No PX4/Gazebo SITL was executed in AeroForge CI.

## Qualification procedure

1. Preserve manufacturer product identifiers, requested bundle, delivered SKUs and actual dated document revisions.
2. Download the original manufacturer STEP and record its hash, unit convention, assembly component positions and registered motor centers.
3. Check dimensional interference for props, payload mounts, ESCs, power and sensor envelopes. Do not infer motor coordinates from wheelbase alone.
4. Verify the exact controller/power-module pair, pin-level wiring, regulated power budget and connector polarity.
5. Measure the integrated airframe mass, symmetric positive-definite inertia, RPM/thrust/current curve and motor dynamic response.
6. Configure tested PX4 firmware, actuator mapping, telemetry regulatory variant, RC failsafe and recovery behavior.
7. Separately compare calibrated SITL and physical telemetry under documented controlled tests before any further engineering release.

Checkboxes are user attestations, not records of independently measured facts. This v0.6 release authorizes neither purchase nor battery charging, powered operation, test flight or autonomous deployment.
